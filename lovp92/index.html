Figure 4I, checked independently

The functional subcircuit is LoVP92 → VES200m → DNg13. The paper says LoVP92 drives VES200m bilaterally and VES200m inhibits DNg13 ipsilaterally. Both hold. LoVP92 to VES200m splits across the midline almost evenly (L→L 196, L→R 187, R→L 110, R→R 194). VES200m to DNg13 is strictly one sided: 167 and 194 within a hemisphere against 1 and 0 across it. LoVP92 also contacts DNg13 directly, but at 54 synapses against 362 through VES200m, the inhibitory route dominates.

Verdict

What the circuit lets you manipulate

A line counts as specific only when its type is the single strongest match across the whole EM volume. Filling every cell of a type is not sufficient: a line can cover a type completely and still label three others as brightly.

Where it breaks

Lines were found by colour depth matching the male CNS connectome against the FlyLight light-microscopy libraries, independently of the paper's own choices. Every cell of every type shown here was matched; two types not shown on this page, TmY21 and Tm26, were sampled rather than matched in full. PatchPerPix does not exist for the male CNS dataset, so this is colour depth matching only. Hemidriver halves come from the FlyLight Split-GAL4 collection, which publishes genotypes for curated SS and OL lines but not for Omnibus Broad IS lines. No stock availability is asserted: BDSC blocks programmatic access, so every line and fragment needs checking by hand before ordering. Neurotransmitters are the connectome's predictions, not measurements.
