Unilateral in T1: AN05B102b, AN05B102c. All other types receive T1 input on both sides. AN05B102a is recorded in the maleCNS synonyms annotation as PPN1 (Kallman 2015).

refGen1 MCFO: R56C09 – the line used for PPN1 in Kallman et al. 2015. The labelled morphology looks like AN05B102a.

On the PPN1 identification. The name comes from Kallman, Kim & Scott 2015, who described an ascending neuron downstream of female pheromone-sensing neurons and drove it with the Janelia line R56C09. From there the identification forks. FlyBase’s anatomy ontology term FBbt:00111517 (“adult pheromone projection neuron 1”) lists AN05B023 as an exact synonym of PPN1, and Virtual Fly Brain serves that same term, so anything resolving through VFB inherits that reading. The male CNS connectome says otherwise: the synonyms annotation carries “Kallman 2015: PPN1” on AN05B102a (bodies 11431 and 12286) and on no AN05B023 body. Two further details favour AN05B102a. Neurotransmitter is a hard discriminator that neither resource mentions: AN05B102a is cholinergic, while all eight AN05B023a–d bodies are GABAergic. And the ontology’s stated T3 soma matches no candidate at all – AN05B023a is T2, b is A2, c is A6, d is A9, and AN05B102a is A1 – which suggests that definition text is a loose transcription of the 2015 figure rather than a curated anatomical claim. This page follows the connectome annotation and treats the ontology entry as the error.
