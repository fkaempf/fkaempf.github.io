AVLP743m two-hop circuit

The male CNS type graph around AVLP743m (male-cns:v1.0), drawn as a force layout that is never allowed to settle. 459 cell types, 3547 connections.

Every bubble is a cell type, not a neuron: the 11 AVLP743m bodies are one node, and so are the 13 DA1_lPN bodies. Every wire is a type-to-type connection, and its weight is the share of the target type’s total input that the source supplies — a 5% wire means the postsynaptic type gets a twentieth of everything it receives from that one presynaptic type. The type-pair matrix behind this is thresholded at 5 synapses. The export then keeps a pair only when it reaches both 0.75% of the target’s input and 5 synapses, so nothing weaker exists in the file: an empty stretch of the graph means below the floor, not zero. Two hops means the direct partners of AVLP743m and the partners of those partners, taken in the same direction — inputs of inputs on the left, outputs of outputs on the right.

Drag a bubble to pull the layout around; it keeps jiggling because the simulation is never allowed to reach zero. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag the background to pan. Hover a bubble to isolate it, click to pin it and open its card, double-click to unpin. Raising a threshold removes a type together with everything that only hung off it, so what is left is always still wired to AVLP743m. Every control is written into the address bar, so the link you copy is the view you are looking at.
