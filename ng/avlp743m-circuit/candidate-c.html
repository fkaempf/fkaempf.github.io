AVLP743m two-hop circuit

Every cell type within two steps of AVLP743m in the male CNS (male-cns:v1.0), drawn as a live force graph. The input side runs down the left, the output side down the right, AVLP743m sits in the middle.

A node here is a cell type, not a neuron: the 11 AVLP743m bodies are one node, and so are the 13 bodies of DA1_lPN. An edge is the summed connection between two types, and its weight is the share of the target type’s total input that the edge supplies, so the same 200 synapses are a heavy edge into a small cell and a light one into a large cell. The type-pair matrix behind the graph is thresholded at 5 synapses, and the export keeps a pair only when it reaches 0.75% of the target’s input and 5 synapses; nothing weaker exists in the file, so the sliders can only take away. Two hops means the partners of AVLP743m and the partners of those partners in the same direction: upstream of upstream on the left, downstream of downstream on the right. A node is drawn only while a chain of drawn edges still connects it to AVLP743m, so raising a threshold removes the whole branch and not just the wire.

Colour is the type’s own consensus transmitter, carried by the node and by every wire leaving it, so the sign of a connection reads off the wire rather than off the cell it lands on. Bubble area is the summed weight of the edges currently drawn, in whichever metric is selected, which means a bubble shrinks when a slider takes its edges away. Only edges that run within one direction are drawn: a wire from the input side straight to the output side is outside the two-hop model and is left out. The layout never fully settles — the simulation is held at a low temperature so the bubbles keep drifting; drag one and its neighbours follow. Hover a node to isolate it, click it to pin it and open its card, double-click to let it go. Every control is written into the address bar, so a filtered view is a link you can send.
