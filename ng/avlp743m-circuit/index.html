AVLP743m circuit graph

Two hops of the type-level connectome around AVLP743m, male CNS (male-cns:v1.0). Back to the AVLP743m scenes.

A node is a cell type, not a neuron. The 11 bodies of AVLP743m are one bubble, and so are the 13 bodies of DA1_lPN; every connection drawn here is summed over all bodies of both types. An edge weight is the share of the target’s total input that the source supplies, so the same 200 synapses make a heavy edge into a small cell and a light one into a large cell. Raw synapse count is the second metric, for when you want the other reading.

The type-pair matrix behind this is thresholded at 5 synapses, and the export keeps a pair only if it is at least 0.75% of the target’s input and at least 5 synapses. Nothing weaker than that is in the file, so the sliders cannot open onto connections this page never received. Two hops means partners, and partners of partners in the same direction: left of AVLP743m are its inputs and their inputs, right of it are its outputs and their outputs, and the five columns are named on the figure.

What the drawing means

Bubble area is the summed weight of the wires you can currently see, in whichever metric is selected, so it describes this view and not a fixed property of the type. Wire colour is the transmitter of the presynaptic type, which is the sign of that connection. A type is drawn only while a chain of at most two visible wires still joins it to AVLP743m: raise a threshold or switch off a transmitter, and everything that hung off what disappeared disappears with it. The bound is what keeps the figure honest. Two thirds of the connections in the file run between two second-hop types, and letting those count as steps would leave types three and four wires out sitting in a drawing captioned two.

A wire that runs straight from the input side to the output side is outside the two-hop model. It is drawn faintly whenever both of its ends are already on screen, but it never keeps a type there; only same-direction chains count towards reaching AVLP743m. Shares add up honestly on the incoming side, where every wire is a fraction of the same cell’s input, but on the outgoing side each wire is a fraction of a different target, so the tooltip and the card give the outgoing total in synapses instead.
