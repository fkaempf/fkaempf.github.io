Read this first: AVLP743m is a part of LC1, and no paper names it.

The male CNS annotation gives AVLP743m the old name LC1 (Ruta 2010). It gives the same name to LH004m and LH007m. All three types sit in one hemilineage, VLPl4_dorsal, and LH004m and LH007m release GABA, which agrees with the GABAergic LC1 of Clowney 2015. The transmitter call for AVLP743m is "unclear". So the LC1 call is coherent, but LC1 is a soma cluster of at least three modern types. Ruta 2010 counts LC1 at about 26 cells per hemisphere in males and 16 in females. The three types together hold 13 cells on the left and 12 on the right. Read every LC1 result below as a cluster result, not as a result about AVLP743m.

One conflict stays open, and it is a conflict between methods, not a reason to drop the name. Kohl 2013 Table 1 aligns LC1 with the Cachero clone aSP-k, and the annotation gives aSP-k and aSP8 to LHAV4c2, which is in a different hemilineage (LHl1). Light microscopy joined what the connectome separates.

The older project label vAB3 is wrong. vAB3 is an ascending neuron of the nerve cord. The annotation gives vAB3 to AN09B017f and AN09B017g, which are two of the input partners on this page, and Stuerner 2025 makes the same assignment in print.

No experiment names AVLP743m, and no experiment manipulates LC1.

How to read this page

This page reports the published function of AVLP743m and of its connectome partners. It uses one rank scale for every function statement.

Rank What it means
RANK 1 Causal behaviour. Somebody activated, silenced, ablated or genetically changed the cells, then measured a behaviour.
RANK 2 Physiology. Somebody recorded the cells, or imaged them, and found a stimulus that drives them.
RANK 3 Anatomy or expression. Morphology, cell counts, dimorphism, transmitter, projection pattern. No stimulus and no manipulation.
RANK 4 Connectome only. The cell exists in an EM dataset and has annotated partners. No experiment.

How the search worked. Modern connectome type names (AVLP743m, mAL_m8, SIP101m) almost never occur in papers. The search therefore used the curated old names from the male-CNS connectome synonym table (LC1, aSP-f, mAL, aIP-g, vAB3 and so on). Each old name was searched in the primary literature. A second agent then attacked every claim: it checked that the paper exists, that the quotations are correct, and that the paper supports the claim.

The coarseness rule. One old name can cover many modern types. An old name is usually a MARCM clone, a soma cluster or a lineage. The male-CNS connectome splits such a group into several types. A result that used a driver line for the old name is therefore a property of the GROUP, not of one type. This page marks every such statement as group level and gives the number of modern types that share the old name. Do not read a group-level function as a function of one type.

Preprints. Some sources are preprints. They are not peer reviewed. This page marks them.

AVLP743m

No function is published for AVLP743m. No person has activated, silenced, ablated or recorded this cell type.

The male-CNS connectome gives AVLP743m one curated old name: "Ruta 2010: LC1". The connectome record is: 11 cells (6 left, 5 right), male-specific with no female counterpart, transmitter "Unclear", superclass cb_intrinsic, hemilineage VLPl4_dorsal, supertype 15598 (RANK 4; Berg 2025 / male-CNS resource, verified live).

The LC1 call is coherent, but LC1 is a cluster. LC1 is a soma-position cluster name from Ruta et al. 2010 ("lateral cluster 1"). The male CNS curation gives that name to three types, and all three sit in one hemilineage:

Type Cells Transmitter Hemilineage Curated old name
AVLP743m 11 (6 L, 5 R) unclear VLPl4_dorsal LC1
LH004m 6 (3 L, 3 R) GABA VLPl4_dorsal LC1, fru-M-500251
LH007m 8 (4 L, 4 R) GABA VLPl4_dorsal LC1, fru-M-300124

Two facts support the call. The two sibling types release GABA, and Clowney et al. 2015 call LC1 GABAergic and inhibitory. The three types share one hemilineage, which is what a light-level soma cluster should map onto.

Two facts limit it, and both are limits on precision, not reasons to drop the name:

  1. LC1 holds more than one type. Kohl et al. 2013 Table 1 gives Ruta's own LC1 counts as 25.8 (SD 3.4) cells per hemisphere in males and 15.8 (SD 3.0) in females. The three types together hold 13 cells on the left and 12 on the right. So an LC1 result is a result about a mixed group. AVLP743m is one part of it.
  2. One light-level table disagrees with the connectome. Kohl et al. 2013 Table 1 aligns LC1 with the Cachero clone aSP-k, and Clowney et al. 2015 write "inhibitory LC1 neurons (also named aSP-k or aSP8)". The male CNS curation gives aSP-k and aSP8 to LHAV4c2, which is in hemilineage LHl1, not VLPl4_dorsal. A MARCM clone and a soma cluster are different objects, and the connectome separates what the light-level work joined. This page follows the connectome.

What would settle the remaining question: a transmitter call for AVLP743m, and a direct comparison of the soma positions of AVLP743m, LH004m and LH007m with the LC1 somata of Ruta et al. 2010.

Therefore this page reports LC1 results as group-level results of a cluster of at least three types. It does not attach them to AVLP743m alone.

What is published under the name LC1:

  • Ruta 2010 (RANK 2, group level, LC1 cluster) - Ruta et al. measured a response of LC1 to stimulation of the DA1 glomerulus. Two independent secondary sources record this: Kohl et al. 2013 Table 1 gives LC1 "DA1 Stim. Response male = +++", and Clowney et al. 2015 state that "LC1 and DC1 neurons are known to be coactivated upon DA1 stimulation (Ruta et al., 2010)". Important limit: the same Kohl table gives the LC1 cVA response as "n/a". The drive was artificial glomerulus stimulation, not the odour cVA. A dimorphic pheromone circuit in Drosophila from sensory input to descending output, Nature. link. The full text is paywalled and was not read; Unpaywall reports the article as closed.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 3, group level, LC1 cluster) - PA-GFP photolabelling shows that the LC1 and DC1 populations both send projections into the lateral protocerebral complex. The authors state that they could not see LC1: "While we were unable to visualize GABAergic LC1 neurons in our preparation". This paper contributes anatomy for LC1 and no physiology. Multimodal Chemosensory Circuits Controlling Male Courtship in Drosophila, Neuron. link.
  • Fisher 2024 (RANK 3, group level, LC1 cluster, preprint) - Disynaptic anterograde tracing (ds-Tango) from Or67d sensory neurons labels LC1 in both sexes, while DC1 and DC2 are labelled in males only. Not all cells of the lateral clusters are FruM-positive. No function was tested. Convergent olfactory circuits for courtship in Drosophila revealed by ds-Tango, bioRxiv. link.

Verdict on LC1 versus vAB3. An earlier file in this project labelled AVLP743m as "vAB3". That label is wrong. vAB3 is an ascending neuron: Clowney et al. 2015 write that "vAB3 neurons innervate only the first thoracic ganglion" and that the projections "terminate in a discrete region of the sub-esophageal zone (SEZ) and in the lateral protocerebral complex". The male-CNS connectome gives the synonym vAB3 to AN09B017f and AN09B017g, which are ASCENDING NEURONS with somata in neuromere A10 and one cell per hemisphere. AVLP743m is cb_intrinsic in hemilineage VLPl4_dorsal and has no nerve-cord arbor. A central-brain-intrinsic AVLP cell cannot be an ascending neuron. LC1 stays as the curated old name, and the hemilineage, the sibling transmitters and the cluster size all agree with it. Note that this rejection of vAB3 uses connectome annotation. No paper mentions AVLP743m.

No causal manipulation of AVLP743m exists. No causal manipulation of the LC1 cluster exists either. The one causal experiment in this part of the lateral horn was done on the neighbouring LC2 cluster (see CB1852 below).

Input partners

DA1_lPN - aDT-e (Cachero 2010); "DA1 PN", "DA1 lPN"

7.4% of AVLP743m input.

DA1_lPN is the second-order cVA neuron. It is the best-evidenced input to AVLP743m. Taisz et al. 2023 manipulated this type with a type-specific split-GAL4 line, so its RANK 1 statements are type level. Older papers say "DA1 PN" and treat the whole glomerulus; those statements are group level, because Taisz et al. later split the DA1 output into lPN, lvPN and vPN, and showed that lPN and lvPN do different things.

  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 1, type level) - Optogenetic activation (CsChrimson) of DA1 lPNs in males lowered courtship of females and strongly lowered copulation rate. lPN activation, but not lvPN activation, strongly lowered male-male courtship; ablation of lPNs also lowered male-male courtship. In virgin females, lPN activation had no effect on mating, and lPN ablation did not lower mating success. The authors state: "lPN manipulations recapitulated only the courtship suppressing effects of cVA in male flies with no effect on female sexual behavior." Generating parallel representations of position and identity in the olfactory system, Cell. link.
  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 1, type level) - Coincidence requirement, and negative results. Activation of Ir94e gustatory receptor neurons alone did not change virgin female receptivity. Activation of DA1 lPNs alone did not change it either. Simultaneous activation of both raised receptivity. Separately, lPN activation did NOT raise male-male aggression (lvPN activation did, moderately), and neither type changed female-female aggression. Same paper. link.
  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 2, type level) - Two-photon calcium imaging of DA1 lPN axons, with a real male fly on a micromanipulator. lPNs report the distance of the male with a tuning curve that plateaus at 1 mm, while the receptor neurons still respond reliably at 5 mm. lPNs report the angular position of the male from the concentration difference between the two antennae. A block of the contralateral antenna lowered the bilateral contrast in lPNs from 42% dF/F difference to 11%. The source of the contralateral inhibition is il3LN6, a GABAergic bilateral local neuron that receives 5 times more synapses from contralateral than from ipsilateral receptor neurons. Same paper. link. Limit stated by the authors: the imaged region contains almost only DA1 lPN axons, but a few other projection neuron types (DL3, VA1v, VA1d) have arbors nearby.
  • Kohl 2013 (RANK 2, group level - the whole DA1 projection neuron population, not lPN alone) - Local acetylcholine iontophoresis onto DA1 projection neuron dendrites produced spiking and large depolarisations in all male aSP-f neurons and in almost all female aSP-g neurons; male aSP-g neurons did not respond. Latency was 1.8 +/- 0.4 ms, short enough to call the connection monosynaptic. In Or67d-null flies, the cVA-evoked responses were abolished. Note that the recording is from the lateral horn neuron. The projection neuron is the stimulated, unrecorded side, and the stimulus excites every DA1 projection neuron class at once. A bidirectional circuit switch reroutes pheromone signals in male and female brains, Cell. link.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 2, group level - the DA1 glomerulus, a mixed receptor-neuron and projection-neuron signal) - In tethered walking males, the DA1 glomerulus was excited as soon as a male target fly came within 1-2 body lengths (3-6 mm) of the antennae. Virgin female stimuli produced little DA1 excitation. Acetylcholine stimulation of the DA1 glomerulus caused little P1 excitation by itself, but it lowered vAB3-driven activation of P1. Virgin females perfumed with synthetic cVA caused much lower P1 responses, and this suppression was weaker in Or67d-mutant males. Multimodal Chemosensory Circuits Controlling Male Courtship in Drosophila, Neuron. link.
  • Datta 2008 (RANK 3, group level - the whole DA1 projection neuron population) - PA-GFP photoactivation showed that the projections from the DA1 glomerulus to the protocerebrum are sexually dimorphic. There is a male-specific axonal arbor in the lateral horn, and its elaboration needs expression of the FruM transcription factor in DA1 projection neurons and other fru+ cells. Anatomy and genetics only. The Drosophila pheromone cVA activates a sexually dimorphic neural circuit, Nature. link. Later disagreement: Kohl et al. 2013 argue that this male-specific axon extension "cannot be sufficient for rewiring".

M_lvPNm45 - no old light-level name (= DA1 lvPN of Taisz 2023)

6.5% of AVLP743m input.

This type is one-to-one with the DA1 lateroventral projection neuron of Taisz et al. 2023, so its claims are type level. The name is a misnomer: the "m" prefix is a legacy hemibrain label for multiglomerular, but Taisz et al. show the cell is uniglomerular.

  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 1, type level) - Optogenetic activation of lvPNs in females raised copulation rate, which the authors read as higher female receptivity. Ablation of lvPNs, but not of lPNs, lowered mating success. Activation of lvPNs, but not of lPNs, moderately raised aggression between pairs of males. Generating parallel representations of position and identity in the olfactory system, Cell. link.
  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 2, type level) - Functional imaging with a live male fly as the stimulus: "lvPN responses are smaller at mid-range distances (5-2 mm) but grow sharply and without saturation from 2 to 0.25 mm", and lvPNs "showed no adaptation throughout the 10-s stimulus". The driving stimulus is the male pheromone cVA. lvPN and lPN are therefore parallel excitatory cVA channels with different dynamics: lPN reports angular position, lvPN reports proximity tonically. Same paper. link.
  • Taisz 2023 (RANK 3, type level) - "we found a uniglomerular cell type from the lateroventral lineage, which we call DA1 lateroventral projection neurons (lvPN)", and "DA1 lvPNs receive 99% of their sensory input from Or67d ORNs and make the same axonal projections in both sexes". They bypass the mushroom body and project to the lateral horn and to the superior intermediate protocerebrum. They synapse onto 11 downstream cell types, and "lvPN targets were predominantly multimodal rather than olfactory (7 vs. 2)". Same paper. link.

Caution: the female receptivity result was obtained in females. Do not transfer it to the male AVLP743m circuit.

AN09B017f - vAB3 (Yu 2010)

5.0% of AVLP743m input.

vAB3 is the ascending relay that carries female contact-pheromone signal from the foreleg to the courtship-command neurons P1. Group level. The old name vAB3 covers more than one modern ascending type. Berg et al. 2025 state that AN09B017 is one of three serially homologous ascending cell types "each containing multiple subtypes recorded in neuprint". Stürner et al. 2025 give the modern mapping in hedged words only ("AN09B017 seems to match fru-expressing vAB3 neurons"), and the match is to the AN09B017 group, not to a lettered subtype. No publication separates AN09B017f from AN09B017g.

  • von Philipsborn 2014 (RANK 1, group level) - A micro-RNAi against the fruitless C isoform was driven by pox9-1-6-GAL4, which labels vAB3. The vAB3 arborisations became female-like, and the males lost pulse song in the dark. The abstract states: "Removal of Fru(C) from the neuronal classes vAB3 or aSP4 leads to cell-autonomous feminization of arborizations and loss of courtship in the dark." Cellular and behavioral functions of fruitless isoforms in Drosophila courtship, Current Biology. link. Two limits, both from the paper itself: pox9-1-6-GAL4 also expresses in "a few additional neuronal classes", and only the morphology of those classes was controlled, never their function; and the knockdown also "moderately reduced" copulation success in the light. The interpulse interval was normal.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 2, group level) - PA-GFP photoactivation from the axons of ppk25+ foreleg gustatory sensory neurons labelled one ascending class, vAB3. vAB3 innervates only the first thoracic ganglion, so it can receive input only from the foreleg. Its axon bifurcates into the SEZ and the lateral protocerebral complex. In vivo GCaMP imaging in tethered males: "vAB3 neurons responded robustly to foreleg contact with a female abdomen but only weakly to a male", and "Both vAB3 and mAL neurons also responded to synthetic 7,11-HD". Multimodal Chemosensory Circuits Controlling Male Courtship in Drosophila, Neuron. link.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 2, group level) - Stimulation of the ppk25+ terminals at the T1 midline excited vAB3 and mAL within the imaged volume. Direct P2X2 activation of vAB3 gave modest P1 excitation. After a two-photon cut of the mAL axon tract, "P1 responses to vAB3 stimulation increased substantially", and direct activation of mAL also suppressed vAB3-driven P1 excitation. Same paper. link. Three limits from the paper: the map covers "the anterior ~100 um of the brain", not the whole brain; the authors state "our work does not demonstrate monosynaptic connectivity between these neural populations"; and the mAL inhibition was not measured but was inferred by subtraction.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 2, group level) - Negative results. Stimulation of the DA1 glomerulus lowered vAB3-driven activation of P1, but it "neither activated vAB3 neurons nor suppressed direct excitation of this ascending pathway". vAB3 excitation was the same for virgin and mated female stimuli. A cut of the mAL axon tract had no effect on vAB3 activity. The olfactory suppression therefore acts at P1, not on the vAB3 relay. Same paper. link.
  • Yu 2010 (RANK 3, group level) - Origin of the name. vAB3 is one of about 100 fru+ neuron classes in a digital 3D atlas at cellular resolution, with polarity annotation and computed likely connectivity, and 11 anatomical dimorphisms. The paper's abbreviation list gives "AB, abdominal", so the vAB3 somata are in the abdominal ganglion. No function was tested. Cellular organization of the neural circuit that drives Drosophila courtship behavior, Current Biology. link.
  • Coleman 2024 (RANK 3, group level) - Restatement plus new anatomy. The paper states that in D. melanogaster males, ppk25+ sensory neurons responsive to 7,11-HD relay these signals to both P1 subtypes through the vAB3 ascending pathway, citing earlier work. For D. yakuba the authors write only that vAB3 "may" convey 7-T excitation to the Dsx+/Fru- P1 subpopulation, which is speculation. The paper adds new PA-GFP labelling of vAB3 (Extended Data Fig. 10c), so it contributes anatomy, not physiology. A modular circuit coordinates the diversification of courtship strategies, Nature. link.

Two mis-attributions to reject: Kallman, Kim and Scott 2015 tested PPN1, not vAB3. von Philipsborn et al. 2011 (courtship song) did not test vAB3.

AN05B102d - no old name

4.7% of AVLP743m input (listed here for completeness; it is an input partner).

No function published. No paper records from AN05B102d, activates it, silences it or names it. Searches for AN05B102, AN05B102d and AN_multi_69 returned no paper that studies this cell.

There is no old name, so there is no name-level coarseness. The coarseness is at the hemilineage level and it is severe. Hemilineage 05B contains many neuron types in every thoracic segment, mostly local and intersegmental interneurons. AN05B102d is one ascending type inside it. A further caution: the nerve-cord annotation merged the old "05A" into "05B" as a minority transmitter subpopulation, so "5B" in Harris et al. 2015 and "05B" in the connectome annotations are not guaranteed to be the same set of cells.

  • Harris 2015 (RANK 1, HEMILINEAGE LEVEL ONLY - not a function of this type) - dTRPA1 was expressed in the thoracic hemilineage 5B and the flies were heated. "Decapitated flies showed a progressive repositioning of their limbs so that they are closer to the ground". "Tethered intact flies that were gripping a Styrofoam ball responded... by lowering their body position, thereby drawing the ball closer to them". "Spontaneous grooming appeared to be suppressed and we observed no walking or wing associated behaviors". Anatomy: "5A undergoes programmed cell death leaving only the 5B interneurons"; the somata are ventrolateral; the axons "cross the midline in the iP commissure, and form a major ipsilateral arbor just prior to crossing", and the contralateral arbor "extends up and down the medial region of the neuropil and into the neck connective". 5B is GABA-immunopositive and the authors say it "may have a general suppressive function". This manipulation covers dozens of cell types across the thoracic neuromeres. It is NOT a function of AN05B102d. Neuron hemilineages provide the functional ground plan for the Drosophila ventral nervous system, eLife. link.
  • Schlegel 2024 (RANK 4) - The FlyWire whole-brain annotation paper is the source of the "AN_" naming scheme ("602 new AN types ("AN_" or "SA_" prefix, for example, AN_SMP_1)") and of the hierarchical class, cell-type and hemilineage annotation. It attributes no function to any AN type. Note two things: the exact string "AN_multi_69" does not appear in the article text, so the mapping AN05B102d = AN_multi_69 is unverified here; and this paper annotates the FlyWire brain dataset, while hemilineage names such as AN05B102 come from the nerve-cord annotation. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila, Nature. link.
  • Stürner 2025 (RANK 4) - The complete connectomic description of the ascending and descending neurons of the neck connective, with all ascending populations classified. The paper analyses named reproductive circuits (DNa12/aSP22 for courtship, hemilineage 08B ascending neurons for song, DNp13 for ovipositor extrusion). It gives no function for any 05B ascending type, and AN05B102 is never mentioned in it. Comparative connectomics of Drosophila descending and ascending neurons, Nature. link.
  • Berg 2025 (RANK 4, preprint) - AN05B102 types are annotated in the complete male CNS connectome. The paper places AN05B102 among "3 serially homologous cell types... each containing multiple subtypes" that are selective for pheromone-responsive gustatory receptor neuron input. That is a connectome-derived circuit statement, not a function. Sexual dimorphism in the complete connectome of the Drosophila male central nervous system, bioRxiv. link.

AN09B017g - vAB3 (Yu 2010)

4.7% of AVLP743m input.

AN09B017g carries the same old name as AN09B017f. The published record is identical, and it is group level. Nothing in the literature separates the two types. There is a further problem: Clowney's vAB3 has dendrites confined to T1 and somata in the abdominal ganglion, while Berg et al. 2025 state that AN09B017 has multiple subtypes in different neuromeres. At most one AN09B017 subtype can be the vAB3 that Clowney recorded. The same function cannot be true of both f and g.

  • von Philipsborn 2014 (RANK 1, group level) - Same claim and same limits as under AN09B017f. Cellular and behavioral functions of fruitless isoforms in Drosophila courtship, Current Biology. link.
  • Clowney 2015 (RANK 2, group level) - Same three physiology claims as under AN09B017f, with the same limits. Multimodal Chemosensory Circuits Controlling Male Courtship in Drosophila, Neuron. link.
  • Yu 2010 (RANK 3, group level) - Origin of the name vAB3. Anatomy, polarity and predicted connectivity only. Cellular organization of the neural circuit that drives Drosophila courtship behavior, Current Biology. link.

Group level throughout. aSP-f is a cluster. Kohl et al. 2013 filled 37 male aSP-f cells. The male-CNS connectome splits the cluster into at least LH008m and LH001m, and no publication separates them. 2.1% of AVLP743m input.

The published male result for this old name is a negative result. Kohl et al. 2013 recorded male aSP-g and found no cVA response and no response to DA1 projection neuron stimulation. AVLP750m is a male-specific type. A null is not a function.
