Every publication found for each input and output cell type of AVLP743m, with the old name that produced the hit.

Function Publications Scenes and partner tables

Read this first: AVLP743m is a part of LC1, and no paper names it.

The male CNS annotation gives AVLP743m the old name LC1 (Ruta 2010). It gives the same name to LH004m and LH007m. All three types sit in one hemilineage, VLPl4_dorsal, and LH004m and LH007m release GABA, which agrees with the GABAergic LC1 of Clowney 2015. The transmitter call for AVLP743m is "unclear". So the LC1 call is coherent, but LC1 is a soma cluster of at least three modern types. Ruta 2010 counts LC1 at about 26 cells per hemisphere in males and 16 in females. The three types together hold 13 cells on the left and 12 on the right. Read every LC1 result below as a cluster result, not as a result about AVLP743m.

One conflict stays open, and it is a conflict between methods, not a reason to drop the name. Kohl 2013 Table 1 aligns LC1 with the Cachero clone aSP-k, and the annotation gives aSP-k and aSP8 to LHAV4c2, which is in a different hemilineage (LHl1). Light microscopy joined what the connectome separates.

The older project label vAB3 is wrong. vAB3 is an ascending neuron of the nerve cord. The annotation gives vAB3 to AN09B017f and AN09B017g, which are two of the input partners on this page, and Stuerner 2025 makes the same assignment in print.

No experiment names AVLP743m, and no experiment manipulates LC1.

Introduction

This report gives the published literature on the input and output partners of AVLP743m in the Janelia male CNS connectome (male-cns:v1.0).

Where the old names came from. The search did not guess the old names. Every search term came from the curated synonyms column of the male CNS annotation. That column records, for each modern type, the name or names that earlier papers used for the same cell or for the group that contains it. The synonym map below reproduces that column verbatim. The search then looked for each old name in PubMed, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, bioRxiv, publisher full text, the Virtual Fly Brain / FlyBase anatomy ontology, and the neuPrint male-cns:v1.0 type pages.

How the citations were checked. A second agent then attacked every citation. It read the full text where the full text was open. It checked that each paper exists, that each quoted sentence is in the source, and that the paper names the type or one of its synonyms. This report applies the corrections of that pass. It removes the citations that failed. The last section lists each removal and the reason. Nothing was removed silently.

Directness. Each citation is marked DIRECT or INDIRECT. DIRECT means the paper names the type or one of its curated synonyms. INDIRECT means the paper covers the pathway, the lineage, the region or the dataset, but does not name the cell.

Caveat 1: the old names are coarser than the modern types

The old names come from light microscopy. They name clones, clusters and classes. The connectome names single cell types. One old name can therefore cover several modern types.

The four SIP types are more than a naming problem. Two of them are inputs to AVLP743m and two are outputs. The old name aSP-a does not separate the input side from the output side.

The size difference is large. Cachero 2010 counts the aSP-a clone at about 50 to 60 cells per hemisphere in males. Cachero 2010 counts the pMP-f clone at about 21 cells per hemisphere, while AVLP757m has one cell per side. The mAL cluster holds about 30 cells per hemisphere in males and splits into at least eight connectome types.

A paper about an old name is therefore evidence about a group. It is not proof about one modern type. Read every statement below in that way. Where a paper made a measurement on a cluster, the report says so.

Caveat 2: name collisions

Several old names collide with unrelated modern names. Each collision below was found and checked during verification.

  • LC1 and LC2 (Ruta 2010) are lateral horn cluster names. They are not the lobula columnar visual types LC1 and LC2. The male CNS text contains the string LC1 three times, and every one is part of LC10a, a visual type.
  • DC1 (Ruta 2010) is a lateral horn cluster. DC1 is also a standard antennal lobe glomerulus name.
  • pIP5 / pIP-e is not pIP10. pIP10 is the descending song command neuron of von Philipsborn 2011. pIP5 is an unstudied class.
  • aSP2 collides with aSP22, a different fruitless descending neuron. Nine of the ten aSP2 string hits in Deutsch 2025 are aSP22.
  • aSP8: Taisz 2023 writes "the fru+ aSP-g (aSP8) neurons". The Kohl 2013 synonym table and the connectome both give aSP-g = aSP6, and give aSP8 = aSP-k = LC1. Do not follow aSP8 from Taisz 2023 into AVLP743m.
  • DC1 synonymy is not settled. Clowney 2015 glosses DC1 as "asp-j or aSP5". Kohl 2013 and Sato 2023 equate DC1 with aSP-f.

What AVLP743m itself is

Verdict

AVLP743m is not vAB3. Retire the AVLP743m = vAB3 mapping. vAB3 is an ascending neuron of the nerve cord. AVLP743m is intrinsic to the central brain. The curated annotation gives vAB3 to AN09B017f and AN09B017g, which are two of the input partners of AVLP743m, and Stuerner 2025 makes the same assignment in print.

The curated name LC1 holds, but it names a cluster, not this type. The annotation gives LC1 to three types, and all three sit in hemilineage VLPl4_dorsal: AVLP743m (11 cells, transmitter unclear), LH004m (6 cells, GABA) and LH007m (8 cells, GABA). The GABA of the two siblings agrees with the GABAergic LC1 of Clowney 2015. Kohl 2013 Table 1 gives Ruta's LC1 counts as male 25.8 (SD 3.4) and female 15.8 (SD 3.0) cells per hemisphere, while the three types together hold 13 cells on the left and 12 on the right. So LC1 is a group of several types, and AVLP743m is one part of it.

One conflict stays open. Kohl 2013 Table 1 aligns LC1 with the Cachero clone aSP-k, and the annotation gives aSP-k and aSP8 to LHAV4c2, which is in hemilineage LHl1. A MARCM clone and a soma cluster are different objects. The connectome separates what the light-level work joined, and this report follows the connectome.

So the safe statement is: AVLP743m is a male-specific type of the third cVA layer, inside the LC1 cluster, with no name of its own and no function in the literature. No paper names AVLP743m. Every LC1 result below is a cluster result.

Why vAB3 is not AVLP743m

vAB3 is an ascending neuron of the ventral nerve cord. AVLP743m is a brain type. These are different objects. Four independent findings support this.

  • Yu 2010 is the naming source. The verification pass obtained the published PDF and found the string vAB3 35 times, including the Figure 7 legend "Ascending pathways. GAL4 lines used for images in (H) were: leg and genitalia, peb-GAL4; vAB3, pox9-1-6". The paper states "We named these neuronal classes by extending and rationalizing current nomenclature". vAB3 is an ascending class in the source that coined the name.
  • Clowney 2015 states in print, verbatim: "vAB3 neurons innervate only the first thoracic ganglion and should receive selective input only from the foreleg", and "Within the brain, vAB3 projections bifurcate and terminate in a discrete region of the sub-esophageal zone (SEZ) and in the lateral protocerebral complex, a Fru+ neuropil also innervated by P1 neurons".
  • Kallman 2015 repeats that vAB3 "has projections in the first leg neuromere in the VNC, the SEZ and protocerebrum".
  • Stuerner 2025, in Nature, makes the modern mapping in print: "AN09B017 seems to match fru-expressing vAB3 neurons, which transmit contact pheromone signals from the front legs to P1 neurons in the brain". This is the same assignment that the curated annotation makes.

So vAB3 belongs to AN09B017f and AN09B017g, which are two of the input partners of AVLP743m. The annotation is internally consistent.

Two limits on that half of the verdict. Stuerner 2025 hedges with "seems to match". Stuerner 2025 also makes the match at the level of AN09B017, not at the level of the lettered subtypes.

What the evidence for LC1 is, and what it is not

  • Ruta 2010 is the naming source for LC1. The paper is closed access. The verification pass could not read the string LC1 in Ruta's own text. It confirmed the name at second hand from Clowney 2015, which writes "inhibitory LC1 neurons" and "GABAergic LC1 neurons" and cites Ruta 2010.

  • The research pass claimed that Kohl 2013 tabulates LC1 and records a male-only DA1 response for it. The verification pass refuted that claim. The verifier read the full text of Kohl 2013 and found zero occurrences of LC1. The sentence presented as a Kohl quote was not the paper's sentence. The male-only DA1 response in Kohl 2013 belongs to aSP-f / DC1, not to LC1. The citation was therefore removed from this section.

  • The table was read directly, and it settles the question. A separate pass fetched the JATS full text of Kohl 2013 from Europe PMC (PMC3898676) and quoted the two header rows of Table 1. Summary of Studies of fru+ LHNs cell by cell:

    Cachero et al. (2010)   aSP-f   aSP-g   aSP-h   aSP-k   aIP-e
Ruta et al. (2010)      DC1     n/a     DC2     LC1     LC2

    The columns align one to one. aSP-k = LC1 and aIP-e = LC2. The same column gives the LC1 counts as male 25.8 (SD 3.4) and female 15.8 (SD 3.0), and LC2 as male 13.0 (SD 2.8) and female 13.3 (SD 2.1). A footnote on the aSP-k column says "Compare with cluster aSP8 in Yu et al. (2010)". Two independent routes, the Europe PMC XML and the typeset article text, give the same rows, and the fetched file was checked by md5 against a fresh fetch.

  • The aSP-k row is a conflict between methods, not a refutation of LC1. Kohl 2013 makes LC1 = aSP-k = aSP8, and the connectome gives aSP-k and aSP8 to LHAV4c2, which is an input and an output partner of AVLP743m. LHAV4c2 sits in hemilineage LHl1. The three types that carry the LC1 name sit in hemilineage VLPl4_dorsal. A MARCM clone (aSP-k) and a soma cluster (LC1) are different objects, and they can overlap without being equal. The connectome separates them by lineage, and this report follows the connectome.

  • The GABA constraint is met at cluster level. Clowney 2015 calls LC1 GABAergic and inhibitory. Of the three types that carry the LC1 name, LH004m and LH007m are GABA in the connectome. The call for AVLP743m is "unclear", which is a missing call, not a contrary call. A transmitter call for AVLP743m would settle this line of evidence.

  • Koganezawa 2016 uses "LC1" for a fruitless single-positive cluster in the courtship-versus-aggression switch. That is the lateral horn sense, not the visual sense.

How coarse LC1 is

Ruta 2010 defined five clusters of candidate third-order lateral horn neurons on the cVA pathway. LC1 is one of them. A cluster holds several cells and, in connectome terms, probably several types. AVLP743m is at most one member of LC1. Taisz 2023 shows the scale of the problem: the third layer of the cVA pathway holds 47 cell types, where the light-microscopy era saw a handful of clusters.

Input partners

DA1_lPN - input 7.4%, cholinergic

Identity. Uniglomerular olfactory projection neuron of the lateral antennal lobe lineage. It innervates the DA1 glomerulus, the target of the Or67d receptor neurons that detect cis-vaccenyl acetate (cVA). About seven cells per hemisphere. It carries cVA to the mushroom body calyx and the lateral horn. Its lateral horn axon arbour is dimorphic. Curated synonym aDT-e (Cachero 2010). The type name DA1_lPN is stable across hemibrain, FlyWire and male CNS.

Coarseness. Moderate. Older papers write "DA1 PN" or "DA1 projection neurons". None of them writes "lPN". The DA1 glomerulus is also innervated by a separate GABAergic ventral PN and by multiglomerular PNs, and broad driver lines label mixed sets. Where a paper used photoactivation or single-cell labelling of the lateral cohort, the match to DA1_lPN is tight. Where it used a broad GAL4 line, the match is not tight. Jefferis 2007 uses a different scheme again: its DA1 iPN is the modern DA1_lPN, and its GABAergic vDA1 is the modern DA1_vPN. Swapping those two would swap an excitatory PN for an inhibitory one.

M_lvPNm45 - input 6.5%, cholinergic. No direct literature found

Identity. Multiglomerular antennal lobe projection neuron. The name is a systematic connectome identifier: M_ means multiglomerular, lvPN means the lateroventral PN lineage BAlp4 / ALlv1, m means the medial antennal lobe tract, and 45 is the type number. The annotation gives no old-literature synonym.

No paper studies this cell. The verification pass searched the full text of Bates 2020, Schlegel 2021, Schlegel 2024, Scheffer 2020 and Taisz 2023 for M_lvPNm45. Every running text gives zero hits. The one place the string appears in print is a supplementary table: Supplementary file S5 of Schlegel 2021 lists three right-hemisphere bodies, 757258507, 757591093 and 792023887, as class mPN, tract mALT, lineage lvPN, cell body fiber AVM06. That is a table row with no discussion.

Coarseness. There is no old name to be coarse about. The coarseness runs the other way. Every citation is about a class that holds dozens of modern types. The hemibrain holds about 90 multiglomerular ALPN types.

Collision warning. The same lineage holds the well-studied uniglomerular DA1 lvPN of the cVA pathway. M_lvPNm45 is multiglomerular. They are different cells. A web summary found during checking states that M_lvPNm45 corresponds to the DA1 lvPN. That statement is false.
