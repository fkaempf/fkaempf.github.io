The first cross: PPN1 fill and ppk23 synapse label

What to set up, which lines do it, and what to clean off them first. Stock data from the laboratory snapshot of 20 July 2026 and from the Cambridge Genetics stockist. Connectome values from male-cns:v1.0.

Working page · Florian Kämpf · 13 August 2026 · Related pages: receptor typing, do the targets group the bristle types, bristle impact.

1 · What the fly must show

One fly must show two things at the same time. The membrane of PPN1 must carry one colour. The presynaptic sites of the ppk23 sensory neurons must carry the other colour. You then count the puncta that touch the membrane.

2 · Two paths, and why both stay open

The synapse label can sit in either binary expression system. The choice is not a preference. It decides which question the animal can answer, and the two questions are different.

PathLabelTargetWhat it buysWhat it costs
A, label in GAL4LMB-494, UAS-brp-D3-Cherry LexA, so R56C09-lexA Both ppk arms run in one reporter configuration, so the two arms can be compared at all. Gr32a also runs. The target is limited to PPN1, the one LexA driver line that exists, and PPN1 takes near equal input from the two groups. Path A therefore develops the assay. It does not test the assignment. See section 6.
B, label in LexAGJ948, LexAop2-Brp-mCherry GAL4, so any GAL4 or split-GAL4 The target can be a split-GAL4. SS103018 labels AN13B002, a discriminating target. The four vAB3 lines become reachable. Only the ppk23 arm exists, because no ppk25-LexA line exists. No ratio.

Path A answers "how much does each ppk group give to PPN1". Path B answers "does this sensory class reach a target that PPN1 cannot separate". Neither replaces the other, so request both lines and build both.

the one snag LexAop2-myr4xSNAPf carries no visible marker. In G0 its presence is deducible, because a fly that is not Tubby must have taken the chromosome 3 that is not TM6B. After that the insertion and a plain chromosome 3 cannot be told apart by eye. Establishing Parent 2 therefore needs one check by PCR, or one stain with a SNAP ligand on a few flies. This is a single cost at the start. Every cross after it is scored by markers alone.

The alternative avoids the stock and the check. Use the G0 progeny directly: R56C09-lexA/CyO; myr4xSNAPf/+ is unambiguous in that generation. The cost is that half the final animals then lack the fill and must be discarded at the microscope, so the useful yield falls from one in three to one in six, and half the animals that look right lack the fill. Build the stock if you will run this more than twice.

3.3 The experimental cross

Parent 1 virgin females  x  Parent 2 males
   keep the male progeny that are not Curly and not Tubby

Both parents are homozygous on chromosome 2, so every progeny carries ppk23-GAL4 / R56C09-lexA and only chromosome 3 must be scored. Not Tubby gives brp-D3-Cherry / myr4xSNAPf. TM6B over TM6B dies, so three classes survive and one in three of the survivors carries all four parts. Half are male, so plan on one useful male in six. Take males only. The midline crossing of the ppk23 neurons in T1 is male specific, and every prediction from the connectome comes from a male.

time Parent 1 takes 3 generations, about 5 weeks, because chromosome 2 is made homozygous. Parent 2 takes the same plus the verification. They run in parallel. The experimental cross takes about 2 weeks. About 7 weeks to the first ppk23 image.

Parent 2 serves every arm. Parent 1 does not: the ppk25 arm needs 4 weeks to clean BDSC 93028, then a rebuild of Parent 1, then the cross. Plan the ppk25 arm as a second block, not as a follow-on week.

5 · Controls, in the same batch

  • Parent 2 crossed to a stock with no GAL4, so the animal carries the target driver, the fill and the label but no ppk23-GAL4. The label channel on the PPN1 mask must be empty. This is the control that can fail, and it sets the threshold below which a punctum count means nothing.
  • Parent 1 crossed to a stock with no LexA. The fill channel must be empty.
  • A neuron with arbors in the same neuropil that the connectome says takes almost no ppk input, filled in the same batch. Its puncta for each square micrometre of membrane is the rate at which apposition is not a synapse. Without it, "touching" is not a measurement.
  • The PPN1 driver with a fill only. This gives the shape of PPN1 on its own, which you need before you can say that a punctum touches it.
  • A female of the full genotype. The ppk23 projection differs between the sexes, so a female is a negative for the male specific part of the pattern.

6 · Why the two arms must share one reporter

One arm on its own gives a number with an unknown efficiency inside it. Comparing two arms removes the part of that efficiency which is common to both, and the two arms can only be compared if they carry the same reporter in the same system. This is a necessary condition, and it is not a sufficient one: see section 11 for what a single target still cannot do.

ppk23 connections onto PPN1, in the connectome27267 a count of connections
presynaptic sites apposed to PPN1about 27000 One site serves about six partners, but those partners are different cells. Onto one target cell a site gives about one connection, so the count of sites is close to the count of connections. Do not divide by six here.
puncta to expect in the count13000 to 20000 on two PPN1 cells together, if the method recovers one half to three quarters
the same for the ppk25 arm11075 connections; the second arm of the comparison
median distance to the nearest site294 nm a confocal microscope resolves about 250 nm

a low count is the failure, not the result Expect of order 20000 puncta over the two PPN1 cells. A few thousand means that the label, the antibody or the segmentation is losing most of the sites. It does not mean that the connectome is wrong. Decide the threshold that you will call a failure before you image.

9 · The expansion step

The sites sit at the limit of a confocal microscope, so the count needs a gel.

FactorMedian distance after the gelVerdict
1, no gel294 nm the sites are not separate
41175 nm enough; 0.1 percent of pairs stay under 250 nm
8 to 202351 to 5877 nm margin, not need

Choose one chemistry and follow it. proExM anchors the protein with Acryloyl-X and digests with proteinase K, and it keeps a usable part of the fluorescence of an expressed protein. A chemistry that denatures by heat preserves the ultrastructure instead and ends that fluorescence, and an antibody must then carry the signal. The two routes are not interchangeable, and section 3 of the receptor typing page gives the same conflict for GRASP.

The antibody route is the safer default because it works with either chemistry. The laboratory holds seven anti-GFP antibodies across four host species and nc82 against Bruchpilot as an independent landmark.

check before staining Neither of the two anti-RFP vials has a host species in the laboratory record, and one is marked low confidence. A two colour stain needs two different hosts, so read the host off both vials and write it into the record before you plan the staining.

no cyanine dyes The gel destroys them, and Alexa Fluor 647 is a cyanine dye. So are Alexa Fluor 555 and DyLight 550, which are of the same sulfoindocyanine class, and the laboratory holds both. Do not use them either.

Use CF488A or an Atto conjugate in the green channel. Use Alexa Fluor 594 or 633 in the red channel. Both are in the laboratory and neither is a cyanine.

The chemical tag fill of section 2 helps here: a SNAP or CLIP tag is smaller and brighter than a fluorescent protein and it survives the chemistry better.

9.1 To order

ItemWhyNote
Acryloyl-X, SEanchors protein and antibody into the gel no substitute; store dry and cold
sodium acrylatethe monomer that swells degrades on the shelf; buy fresh and reject a batch that is not white
acrylamide, bis-acrylamide, APS, TEMED, 4-hydroxy-TEMPOthe rest of the gel standard, and possibly already held
a cell-permeable benzylguanine dye, for example SNAP-Cell 647-SiR or SNAP-Cell TMR-Stardevelops the SNAP fill of path A; without it the target channel stays dark Blocking. A Surface ligand is the wrong class: it cannot cross the membrane and these neurons are inside the nerve cord. Pick a dye that is not a cyanine and that does not sit in the mCherry channel.

the 4x verdict is not established The distances above have a floor at 216 nm, which is 27 voxels of the volume. That floor is how the sites were annotated, not biology, so the fraction of pairs closer than 216 nm is unmeasured. Treat "0.1 percent under 250 nm" as a lower bound. If many pairs sit at the floor, 4x is not enough and a 10x gel is the safe choice.

Take the protocol from the proExM paper of Tillberg and colleagues. This page gives the decisions and the parts, not the recipe.

10 · Order of work

  • The request to the Cambridge Genetics stockist went on 13 August 2026 for LMB-494, GJ948 and LMB-495. Section 1b holds the three and the two questions that are still open. LMB-494 is the one that matters. A vial of GJ948 is also in tray 2 at incubator 18, so path B is not waiting on the shipment.
  • Order the benzylguanine dye of section 9.1 in the same week. It blocks the target channel of path A, so it belongs on the critical path with the gel reagents.
  • Start the two clean-up crosses of section 4 in the same week. Each takes about 4 weeks, and each blocks one later arm.
  • Set up the two parent stocks. Parent 1 takes about 4 weeks and Parent 2 about 5, because of the verification step.
  • Order Acryloyl-X and sodium acrylate in the same week. They arrive long before the flies.
  • Practise the gel on a ventral nerve cord you do not care about. The first gel always fails. These sensory neurons have no presynapse in the brain, so every synapse counted here is in the nerve cord, and the brain is useful only to check the driver pattern.
  • Measure the expansion factor on every gel with a landmark of known size. The factor varies between gels, and every count depends on it.

11 · What this settles, and what it does not

The ppk23 arm alone gives the count, the working dilutions, the imaging settings and the true undercount of the method. Every one of those is needed before the ratio experiment is worth running.

The ppk25 arm at PPN1 gives the variance between animals, the working dilutions and the recovery of the method. It does not test the receptor assignment. PPN1 takes near equal input from the two groups, at a predicted ratio of 0.406 against 0.594 if the assignment is swapped, which is a separation of 1.46 times. The receptor typing page gives the targets that do separate them, and the driver lines that they still need.

The unknown efficiency of the method does not cancel in a single ratio either. It cancels in a ratio of ratios: the same two drivers at two targets whose predicted fractions are opposed. One target alone leaves the driver strength and the staining efficiency inside the number. CROSSING_REVIEW.md sets out the two estimators.

limit No experiment on this page names the sex that a bristle detects. It measures how much of a target's input comes from a labelled sensory class.
