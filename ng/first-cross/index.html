The first cross: PPN1 fill and ppk23 synapse label

What to set up, which lines do it, and what to clean off them first. Stock data from the laboratory snapshot of 20 July 2026 and from the Cambridge Genetics stockist. Connectome values from male-cns:v1.0.

Working page · Florian Kämpf · 13 August 2026 · Related pages: receptor typing, do the targets group the bristle types, bristle impact.

1 · What the fly must show

One fly must show two things at the same time. The membrane of PPN1 must carry one colour. The presynaptic sites of the ppk23 sensory neurons must carry the other colour. You then count the puncta that touch the membrane.

no order needed Every part is in the laboratory or at the Cambridge Genetics stockist. Nothing must be built.

2 · Two paths, and why both stay open

The synapse label can sit in either binary expression system. The choice is not a preference. It decides which question the animal can answer, and the two questions are different.

PathLabelTargetWhat it buysWhat it costs
A, label in GAL4LMB-494, UAS-brp-D3-Cherry LexA, so R56C09-lexA Both ppk arms run in one reporter configuration, so the ppk25 to ppk23 ratio is possible. Gr32a also runs. The target is limited to the one LexA driver line that exists, which is PPN1.
B, label in LexAGJ948, LexAop2-Brp-mCherry GAL4, so any GAL4 or split-GAL4 The target can be a split-GAL4. SS103018 labels AN13B002, a discriminating target. The four vAB3 lines become reachable. Only the ppk23 arm exists, because no ppk25-LexA line exists. No ratio.

Path A answers "how much does each ppk group give to PPN1". Path B answers "does this sensory class reach a target that PPN1 cannot separate". Neither replaces the other, so request both lines and build both.

Two elements sit on chromosome 2 and two on chromosome 3, one of each pair on each homolog. No recombination is needed, and every element expresses.

why brp-D3 Brp-D3 is the short form of Bruchpilot. It goes to the active zone and it does not disturb it. Full length Bruchpilot forms aggregates, so a full length line gives a count that you cannot trust. LMB-494 also arrives balanced on both chromosomes, with Dfd-YFP on each balancer, so the larvae can be sorted under a fluorescence scope.

Every chromosome in this cross is marked, so no verification step is needed. The chromosome 3 of BDSC 93026 carries TM2 with Ubx or TM6B with Tubby. The chromosome 3 of LMB-494 carries brp-D3 or TM6B with Tubby, Stubble and Dfd-YFP. A fly that is Tubby but shows neither Ubx nor Stubble therefore holds the TM6B of BDSC 93026 over the brp-D3 of LMB-494, which is the class wanted. TM6B over TM6B dies, so that class does not appear.

3.2 Parent 2, the target side

GenCrossScoreResult

F1cross to a chromosome 3 balancer, then sibling cross Curly and Tubby, then verify Parent 2, w; R56C09-lexA/CyO; myr4xSNAPf/TM6B

the one snag LexAop2-myr4xSNAPf carries no visible marker. In G0 its presence is deducible, because a fly that is not Tubby must have taken the chromosome 3 that is not TM6B. After that the insertion and a plain chromosome 3 cannot be told apart by eye. Establishing Parent 2 therefore needs one check by PCR, or one stain with a SNAP ligand on a few flies. This is a single cost at the start. Every cross after it is scored by markers alone.

The alternative avoids the stock and the check. Use the G0 progeny directly: R56C09-lexA/CyO; myr4xSNAPf/+ is unambiguous in that generation. The cost is that half the final animals then lack the fill and must be discarded at the microscope, so the useful yield falls from one in nine to one in eighteen. Build the stock if you will run this more than twice.

3.3 The experimental cross

Parent 1 virgin females  x  Parent 2 males
   keep the male progeny that are not Curly and not Tubby

Not Curly gives ppk23-GAL4 / R56C09-lexA on chromosome 2. Not Tubby gives brp-D3-Cherry / myr4xSNAPf on chromosome 3. One in nine of the survivors carries all four parts. CyO over CyO and TM6B over TM6B both die, so each chromosome gives three surviving classes and not four, and the fraction is one third times one third. Half of those are male, so plan on one useful male in eighteen. Take males only. The midline crossing of the ppk23 neurons in T1 is male specific, and every prediction from the connectome comes from a male.

time Parent 1 takes 2 generations, about 4 weeks. Parent 2 takes 2 generations plus the verification, about 5 weeks, and runs in parallel. The experimental cross takes about 2 weeks. Total about 7 weeks, and both parent stocks then serve every later arm.

5 · Controls, in the same batch

  • Parent 1 alone, with no LexA. The fill channel must be empty.
  • Parent 2 alone, with no GAL4. The label channel must be empty.
  • The PPN1 driver with a fill only. This gives the shape of PPN1 on its own, which you need before you can say that a punctum touches it.
  • A female of the full genotype. The ppk23 projection differs between the sexes, so a female is a negative for the male specific part of the pattern.

6 · Why only path A carries the ratio

One arm on its own does not test the receptor assignment. The test is the ratio of the ppk25 arm to the ppk23 arm at the same target, because the unknown efficiency of the method cancels in a ratio. The two arms must therefore use the same reporter in the same system.

Arrangementppk23 armppk25 armRatio possible
Label in GAL4, target in LexAppk23-GAL4ppk25-GAL4 yes Both arms are identical except for the driver.
Label in LexA, target in GAL4ppk23-LexAno ppk25-LexA exists no The second arm cannot be built.

This is why path A carries the ratio. It puts the label on the side that can be swapped, so the same configuration also runs with Gr32a-GAL4, which reaches a sensory class that no ppk line labels. Path B cannot do this, and it is kept for the targets instead. See section 7.

The targets this path opens are the ones PPN1 cannot reach. PPN1 takes near equal input from the two ppk groups, so it cannot separate them; the receptor typing page lists the discriminating targets, and SS103018, which labels AN13B002, is already in the laboratory. The four vAB3 candidate split-GAL4 lines are also GAL4, and three of the four are marked dead, so check them before planning around them. None has been through MCFO, so nobody knows which AN09B017 member each one labels.

9 · The expansion step

The sites sit at the limit of a confocal microscope, so the count needs a gel.

FactorMedian distance after the gelVerdict
1, no gel294 nm the sites are not separate
41175 nm enough; 0.1 percent of pairs stay under 250 nm
8 to 202351 to 5877 nm margin, not need

Label before the gel. A heat denaturation gel ends the fluorescence of an expressed protein, so an antibody must carry the signal. The laboratory holds seven anti-GFP antibodies across four host species, two anti-RFP, and nc82 against Bruchpilot as an independent landmark. Take a host for one target that differs from the host for the other.

no cyanine dyes The gel destroys them, and Alexa Fluor 647 is a cyanine dye. Use CF488A or an Atto conjugate first. Alexa Fluor 555, 594 and 633 and DyLight 550 are acceptable, and all are in the laboratory.

The chemical tag fill of section 2 helps here: a SNAP or CLIP tag is smaller and brighter than a fluorescent protein and it survives the chemistry better.

10 · Order of work

  • Request LMB-494 and GJ948 from the Cambridge Genetics stockist. LMB-494 is the one that matters.
  • Set up the two parent stocks. They take about 4 weeks and nothing else is on the critical path.
  • Order Acryloyl-X and sodium acrylate in the same week. They arrive long before the flies.
  • Practise the gel on a brain you do not care about. The first gel always fails.
  • Measure the expansion factor on every gel with a landmark of known size. The factor varies between gels, and every count depends on it.

11 · What this settles, and what it does not

The ppk23 arm alone gives the count, the working dilutions, the imaging settings and the true undercount of the method. Every one of those is needed before the ratio experiment is worth running.

The ratio of the ppk25 arm to the ppk23 arm tests the receptor assignment at PPN1. PPN1 takes near equal input from the two groups, so it cannot separate them by itself. The receptor typing page gives the targets that can, and the driver lines that they still need.

limit No experiment on this page names the sex that a bristle detects. It measures how much of a target's input comes from a labelled sensory class.
