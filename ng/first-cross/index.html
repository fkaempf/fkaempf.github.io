1 · The target genotypes

Path A, label in GAL4. Both ppk arms are possible. The target is PPN1 only.

w ; ppk23-GAL4 / GMR56C09-lexA(attP40) ; UAS-brp-D3-Cherry / 13XLexAop2-mCD8::GFP(attP2)

Path B, label in LexA. One arm only. Any GAL4 or split-GAL4 target.

w/Y ; ppk23-LexA / + ; target-GAL4 / UAS-myr::GFP     plus LexAop2-Brp-mCherry

PathArms possibleTargets possibleUse it for
Appk23, ppk25, Gr32aPPN1 only the assay: counts, dilutions, settings, recovery
Bppk23 onlyany GAL4, and split-GAL4 the targets that separate the two ppk groups

path A is the main line The receptor driver lines exist in GAL4. Only four LexA lines exist for the ten receptor genes, and only two of them apply here: ppk23-LexA and Ir52a-LexA. Path A can thus use every receptor driver. Path B can use two.

SystemDrivesColourLine
GAL4the active zones of the sensory neuronsred UAS-brp-D3-Cherry, LMB-494
LexAthe membrane of the target neurongreen 13XLexAop2-mCD8::GFP, BDSC 32203

brp-D3-Cherry is red, thus the fill must be green. The two LexAop fluorophores at the Cambridge Genetics stockist, LMB-536 and LMB-538, are both red. Do not use them for path A. PPN1 receives almost equal input from the two ppk groups, thus path A develops the assay. It does not test the receptor assignment.

The outcross to w1118 at F2 is necessary. GJ954 has TM2 and TM6B on chromosome 3, and GJ1472 has the GAL4 or TM3,Ser. Thus no plain chromosome 3 is present in the cross, and an incross alone gives a stock with a balancer on chromosome 3. That balancer then blocks the target driver in the final cross. Select Ser at F1: a fly with Ser cannot also have the 22B10-GAL4 from that parent.
