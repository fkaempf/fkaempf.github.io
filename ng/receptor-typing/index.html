mCNS male-cns:v1.0

Can we settle the receptor typing experimentally?

Turning the contact-chemosensory receptor assignment from an argument into a measurement. Male CNS connectome, male-cns:v1.0.

Working page · Florian Kämpf · last updated 11 August 2026 · sits alongside AN05B102, AN05B023b/c and AN09B017.

The receptor identity of the contact chemosensory neurons was assigned from their downstream partners. The paper's thesis is what those neurons do downstream. That is a loop, and it is the softest joint in the manuscript. This page lays out a way to cut it: treat the assignment as an inverse problem the connectome already specifies, and solve it with a GRASP measurement that the connectome cannot fake.

Method. All numbers are computed from male-cns:v1.0 fetched directly from neuPrint, unthresholded, and independently reproduced to within |ΔR| ≤ 0.00045 by two methods — neuprintr and raw Cypher aggregation. weight is a synapse count, verified against n.downstream for all 526 sensory bodies. Untyped downstream partners account for 0.38% of synapses and cannot bias any target's R.

Do not use the repo's cached connectivity table for this: it is built with fetch_connectivity(synapse_threshold = 5) (R/data_processing.R:123), which is right for path analysis but wrong for predicting GRASP puncta ratios, since GRASP reports weak contacts too. 63% of edges fall below that cut.

2 · The PPN1 evidence does not hold up

On the PPN1 identity. FlyBase's anatomy ontology (FBbt:00111517) lists AN05B023 as a synonym of "adult pheromone projection neuron 1". That is wrong. The mCNS annotation carries "Kallman 2015: PPN1" on AN05B102a (bodies 11431, 12286) and on no AN05B023 body, and AN05B102a is cholinergic where AN05B023a–d are GABAergic. PPN1 is AN05B102a, as analyses/an_investigation/R/_paths.R:54-58 has it — so the 41% figure below is about PPN1 itself, and the argument is stronger for it.

The manuscript leans on PPN1 as corroboration that WG3 is ppk23+ ppk25+, citing the functional connectivity of Kallman et al. 2015. The connectome disagrees. Pooled input synapses onto AN05B102a = PPN1:

PPN1 receives near-equal input from both families — 41% ppk25+. It is a convergent integrator, not a ppk25+ marker. The "corroborated by PPN1" sentence overstates the data and needs rewriting whatever else happens.

Nor is there a spatial escape: on PPN1 the two families interdigitate across the whole dendrite. Kolmogorov–Smirnov D = 0.02–0.06 between LgLG1a and LgLG1b synapse positions across all three axes, maximum 0.08 on any principal axis; the z-decile ppk25+ fraction stays within 0.35–0.48 with no monotone trend. The distributions are all but identical.

3 · The inversion

Stop treating the receptor call as an input to be defended. Treat it as an unknown to be solved for. The connectome supplies the design matrix; a GRASP experiment supplies the observations.

Why this is not circular

  • C is EM synapse counts — segmentation, not typing inference. It is the one thing in the chain nobody disputes.
  • x is the receptor label — the actual unknown.
  • The receptor driver is the independent variable. ppk25- driver expression defines the molecule directly. That is exactly the quantity in question.
  • Nothing downstream is assumed. The wing→leg co-clustering propagation is not used — it is dissolved, not tested.

The subset trick. ppk25+ cells are a subset of ppk23+ cells, so the ppk23 driver labels both families and the ppk25 driver only one. The ratio of their puncta counts at a target is Rₓ, up to an unknown GRASP-efficiency factor k.

k plausibly varies with the postsynaptic driver, the neuropil and the arbour, so a ratio between two different targets does not cancel it. It cancels exactly only within a compartment, over the same GRN population and the same two drivers — see §4.

4 · What the experiment can actually distinguish

Eight types cannot be identified individually. A linear-inverse treatment looks like it should work — a greedy D-optimal target set reaches rank 8 with condition number 2.5 — but that argument assumes the GRASP efficiency factor k and the puncta–synapse saturation exponent γ are known. Neither is. With both free the design matrix does not identify eight parameters, however well conditioned it looks.

The leg is what matters. The paper is about contact chemosensation — foreleg tapping — so the assignment that has to be correct is the leg one. The wing types are the historical entry point for the typing argument, not the target of the experiment. Targets are therefore scored on R renormalised within the leg types only, which is what a leg-compartment readout measures.

The hypothesis space is four, not two

Framing this as "current assignment versus global swap" was a straw man. Nobody believes the whole assignment is inverted. The live alternatives are the ones where the wing→leg propagation fails:

HypothesisWing call (WG3/WG4)Leg call (LgLG*)
H0 — as publishedrightright
H1 — global swapwrongwrong
H2rightwrong — propagation failed
H3wrongright

A contrast between two different targets is largely blind to H2 and H3. Against H3 a contrast between two different targets separates by only about 1.6×, against a detection floor near 2×.

The design that does work — one pair, two compartments

Take one target pair, AN05B023b and AN05B023c, and score each animal separately in Ov (wing input) and LegNp T1–T3 (leg input). That yields two statistics:

W = [log Y(ppk25,c,Ov) − log Y(ppk23,c,Ov)] − [log Y(ppk25,b,Ov) − log Y(ppk23,b,Ov)]
L = [log Y(ppk25,c,Leg) − log Y(ppk23,c,Leg)] − [log Y(ppk25,b,Leg) − log Y(ppk23,b,Leg)]

Each is a within-compartment ratio-of-ratios: same GRN population, same compartment, same two drivers. k cancels exactly — not approximately, and without assuming it is common across targets. W and L are never compared to each other.

Statisticcall rightcall wrongReads out
W+1.04−1.06 the wing assignment
L+1.07−1.15 the leg assignment

log10 units. sign(W) and sign(L) are two independent binary readouts whose pair maps one-to-one onto {H0, H1, H2, H3} — so this design tests the wing→leg propagation directly, which is the thing that was never tested. It also avoids the small-target problem: the weakest predicted signal is ~400 synapses on a two-cell arbour, not a handful on a twenty-cell one.

And one problem sits upstream of all of it. R's denominator assumes the ppk drivers label exactly the eight assigned types and nothing else. 417 GRN cells across five types have no receptor call at all, and four of the eight assigned types have no molecular support for being ppk23+. Until the drivers are mapped onto connectome types — by HCR-FISH or MCFO, cheaper than the GRASP — the GRASP is not interpretable. If ppk23-GAL4 labels more than 1.3× or fewer than 0.7× the assigned cell count, the denominator is wrong and the GRASP should not be built.

The wing targets fail exactly this test. An independent crosscheck reproduced all 22 R values to within 0.00045 by two methods, then broke them down per body:

INXXX044 — 8 bodies, per-body R spans 0.071 to 1.000, SD 0.41. One body carries 638 of 863 synapses; drop it and R collapses to 0.431, inverting the prediction. AN01B004 — 6 bodies in three instance groups, one pair receives zero ppk input, 170 of 178 synapses come from another. Both unusable. AN17A003's 57× is real and bilaterally consistent (L 0.973, R 0.996), but only 2 of its 6 bodies receive meaningful input, so a type-level driver dilutes it threefold.

So the targets that looked strongest on fold alone are the ones that fail per-body scrutiny, and the leg targets — the ones the biology needs — are the robust ones.

Wing and leg decouple — asymmetrically

Wing types (WG*) synapse in Ov, leg types (LgLG*) in LegNp(T1–T3), all of it in the nerve cord. Targets that take input from only one compartment therefore separate the wing receptor assignment from the leg one — which matters, because the leg assignments were propagated from the wing ones by co-clustering and have never been tested independently.

The two sides are not equally testable. The WG3 side has an excellent wing target — AN17A003, WG3 0.930 against WG4 0.017, R = 0.983. The WG4 side does not: the best WG4-loading pure-wing target is IN11A032_a at WG4 0.749 and only 3.0×. So the wing arm rests on an asymmetric pair, and whether that is enough to determine the wing assignment is one of the questions the running crosscheck is meant to answer.

The leg arm is well covered on both sides: AN09B017b (R 0.090) and IN17A013 (0.129) against AN09B017g (0.896) and IN23B020 (0.899), all pure leg, all ≥ 6.7×.

What to actually run

  • The design. One target pair — AN05B023b + AN05B023c — scored separately in Ov and LegNp. Gives W (wing) and L (leg), each with k cancelling exactly, each flipping sign between hypotheses by more than a log₁₀ unit. This is the whole experiment.
  • Do this first, before any flies. Map the ppk drivers onto connectome types by HCR-FISH or MCFO. Cheaper than the GRASP, and it may answer the question outright. If ppk23-GAL4 labels more than 1.3× or fewer than 0.7× the assigned cell count, the GRASP is not interpretable and should not be built.
  • Reference arm. AN05B102a (PPN1) via R56C09 — 27 267 ppk synapses, both drivers guaranteed to give signal, expected R ≈ 0.41. If the ppk23 arm gives no unambiguous signal here, the assay does not work. Stop.
  • Everything else is exploratory. The wider target list below is useful for choosing follow-ups, not for the primary result. Its top rows are small targets whose large fold-changes may be artifacts — see the status note at the top.

6 · Reagents and crossing scheme

Searched directly against Bloomington on 11 August 2026, and independently confirmed by a second pass over FlyBase and BDSC. Stock numbers below were read off stock records, not inferred.

The binary-system problem, and why it is solved

GRNs are presynaptic and the connectome target is postsynaptic, so the two GRASP halves must be driven by two different binary systems. Both ppk drivers exist only as GAL4 — so the target must be LexA.

Do not use vGlut-LexA as an F-cell proxy. Because ppk25-LexA does not exist, Kallman/Scott used vGlut-LexA and vGlut-QF2 to stand in for the F-cell population. For this experiment that is circular — "glutamate implies ppk25+" is precisely the neurotransmitter prediction under test. The whole point is to drive off the receptor promoter.

Chromosome arithmetic

Both t-GRASP halves in BDSC 79040 sit on chromosome 3 (attP2 and VK00027). That rules out two otherwise-obvious reagents.

  • R56C09-GAL4 is out — attP2 is occupied by the LexAop half. Second independent reason to use the LexA version.
  • Segregate ppk25-GAL4. BDSC 93028 carries insertions on both chr 2 and chr 3. The chr 3 copy collides with t-GRASP, so cross it away and keep the chr 2 insertion alone.
  • This also fixes a dosage confound. Two copies of ppk25-GAL4 against one copy of ppk23-GAL4 would differ in driver strength between the two arms — which directly fakes the readout, since the readout is the ratio between those arms. After segregation both arms carry one copy.
  • No recombination is needed on chr 2. ppk-GAL4 and R56C09-lexA can sit in trans, one on each homolog, and both express normally. Bringing them together is a single cross.

Crossing scheme — reference arm

This builds the PPN1 reference arm (ppk-GAL4 × R56C09-lexA), which validates the reagents but cannot discriminate. The primary design in §4 needs AN05B023b and AN05B023c, and neither has a driver — not at Bloomington, and not in the lab (checked against the flomington inventory: zero hits). Building or finding those two drivers is the critical path, ahead of every cross below.

Still open. Only R56C09 has a confirmed LexA driver, so only the PPN1 reference arm is currently buildable. AN05B023b/c and the other discriminating targets need LexA lines that do not yet exist — a NeuronBridge hunt, then either an existing Gen1 LexA or a split-LexA build. That is the critical path, not the crossing.

Experimental animals must be male: the ppk23 T1 midline crossing is male-specific and fru-dependent, and all the connectome predictions come from the male CNS.

10 · Where this leaves the paper

The headline does not depend on the answer. Wang et al. 2011 showed gustatory pheromone signals are hierarchically dominant to olfactory ones. Nobody has the anatomical substrate for that. The Fig 5 result — mAL subtypes preferentially relaying multimodal sensory information — plausibly is that substrate, and it survives a WG3↔WG4 swap untouched.

So: name types by their connectome names, carry receptor identity as a labelled overlay with explicit confidence tiers, run the swap-robustness pass, and the manuscript cannot be killed by killing the assignment. The GRASP experiment then upgrades the overlay from inference to measurement.

Can we settle the receptor typing experimentally?

Turning the contact-chemosensory assignment from an argument into a measurement.
Florian Kämpf · August 2026

The problem

We inferred the labels from the circuit, then used the labels to explain the circuit.

  • Receptor identity for WG3 / WG4 / LgLG* was assigned from downstream partners — PPN1 for WG3, mAL for WG4.
  • The manuscript's thesis is downstream valence logic.
  • Supporting evidence: neurotransmitter prediction, morphology against GAL4 images, and wing→leg co-clustering.
  • No further connectome analysis can break this loop.

And one leg of it is weaker than we thought

PPN1 takes near-equal input from both families — a convergent integrator, not a marker. The two families also interdigitate across its whole dendrite (KS D ≤ 0.08). That sentence in the draft needs rewriting regardless of everything else.

The move

Stop defending the labels. Solve for them.

Rj = Σi xi·C[i,j] ⁄ Σi C[i,j]

  • C = connectome synapse counts. EM segmentation — not typing inference.
  • x = the receptor labels. The actual unknown.
  • R = measured by GRASP, because ppk25+ cells are a subset of ppk23+ cells: the ppk23 driver labels both families, the ppk25 driver only one.
  • Unknown GRASP efficiency k is common across targets and cancels in ratios. One target tells you nothing; two tell you everything.

"Assignment versus global swap" was a straw man — nobody thinks the whole thing is inverted. H2 and H3 are the live ones, and they are exactly where the wing→leg propagation fails.

One pair, two compartments. AN05B023b vs AN05B023c, scored separately in Ov and LegNp. W = +1.04 / −1.06 reads the wing call; L = +1.07 / −1.15 reads the leg call. Each is a within-compartment ratio-of-ratios, so k cancels exactly. Their signs map one-to-one onto the four hypotheses.

The ask

Three tiers, pick a stopping point.

  • 2 targets — AN05B023b + c. Settles the global M/F swap. 17× and 121×.
  • 4 targets — add INXXX044, IN11A022, IN01B065, AN09B017g. Decouples wing from leg.
  • 8 targets — the D-optimal set. Every type solved individually.
  • Reference arm throughout — R56C09 / PPN1. Without it a negative is uninterpretable.

Main unknown: driver lines. R56C09 is confirmed. AN09B017e/f = vAB3 and AN13B002 = Dandelion are named and may have reagents. The rest need a NeuronBridge hunt.

Crossing

Build one stock, then one cross per target.

  • Stock A = w; ppk-GAL4 ; LexAop-post-tG, UAS-pre-tG. Two generations, ~6–8 weeks. Reused for every target.
  • Each experiment = Stock A ♀ × target-LexA ♂. All F1 males are the experimental genotype — no selection.
  • ppk-GAL4 and R56C09-lexA sit in trans on chr 2. No recombination needed.
  • Segregate the chr 3 copy of ppk25-GAL4 — it collides with t-GRASP, and two copies vs one would fake the ratio that is the whole readout.

Males only: ppk23 T1 midline crossing is male-specific and fru-dependent.

The actual bottleneck

Driver lines for the targets, not the genetics.

Only R56C09 has a confirmed LexA driver — and that is the reference arm, the one that cannot discriminate.

AN05B023b/c and the other discriminating targets need LexA lines that do not yet exist. Route: NeuronBridge colour-depth search from the EM body, then an existing Gen1 LexA if one matches, otherwise a split build.

Risk

The paper is safe either way.

  • Name types by connectome names; carry receptor identity as a labelled overlay with explicit confidence tiers.
