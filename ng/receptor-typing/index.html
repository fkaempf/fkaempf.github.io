Can we settle the receptor typing experimentally?

Turning the contact-chemosensory receptor assignment from an argument into a measurement. Male CNS connectome, male-cns:v0.9.

Working page · Florian Kämpf · last updated 11 August 2026 · sits alongside AN05B102, AN05B023b/c and AN09B017.

The receptor identity of the contact chemosensory neurons was assigned from their downstream partners. The paper's thesis is what those neurons do downstream. That is a loop, and it is the softest joint in the manuscript. This page lays out a way to cut it: treat the assignment as an inverse problem the connectome already specifies, and solve it with a GRASP measurement that the connectome cannot fake.

2 · PPN1 is not the marker the draft says it is

The manuscript leans on PPN1 as corroboration that WG3 is ppk23+ ppk25+, citing the functional connectivity of Kallman et al. 2015. The connectome disagrees. Pooled input synapses onto AN05B102a = PPN1:

AN05B102b1262701019 6745%
AN05B102c35992591482 12429%
AN05B023b67444346 5893988%

PPN1 receives near-equal input from both families. It is a convergent integrator, not a ppk25+ marker. The "corroborated by PPN1" sentence overstates the data and needs rewriting whatever else happens.

Nor is there a spatial escape: on PPN1 the two families interdigitate across the whole dendrite. Kolmogorov–Smirnov D ≈ 0.11–0.26 between LgLG1a and LgLG1b synapse positions — significant only because n is 5574 vs 4650. The distributions almost completely overlap.

3 · The inversion

Stop treating the receptor call as an input to be defended. Treat it as an unknown to be solved for. The connectome supplies the design matrix; a GRASP experiment supplies the observations.

Why this is not circular

  • C is EM synapse counts — segmentation, not typing inference. It is the one thing in the chain nobody disputes.
  • x is the receptor label — the actual unknown.
  • The receptor driver is the independent variable. ppk25- driver expression defines the molecule directly. That is exactly the quantity in question.
  • Nothing downstream is assumed. The wing→leg co-clustering propagation is not used — it is dissolved, not tested.

The subset trick. ppk25+ cells are a subset of ppk23+ cells, so the ppk23 driver labels both families and the ppk25 driver only one. The ratio of their puncta counts at a target is Rₓ. GRASP efficiency differs between the two drivers by an unknown factor k, but k is common across targets and cancels in Rₓ/Rₓ′. Hence: one target tells you nothing, two tell you everything.

5 · Recommended target sets

  • Two targets, maximum power. AN05B023b + AN05B023c. 17× and 121×. Settles the global M/F swap and nothing else.
  • Four targets, wing and leg decoupled. INXXX044 + IN11A022 for the wing; IN01B065 + AN09B017g for the leg. Kills the co-clustering propagation question.
  • Eight targets, full identifiability. The D-optimal set. Every type solved individually, condition number 2.5.
  • Reference arm in all cases. AN05B102a (PPN1) via R56C09 — 27 083 ppk synapses, both drivers guaranteed to give signal, expected R ≈ 0.41. Without it a negative elsewhere is uninterpretable.

10 · Where this leaves the paper

The headline does not depend on the answer. Wang et al. 2011 showed gustatory pheromone signals are hierarchically dominant to olfactory ones. Nobody has the anatomical substrate for that. The Fig 5 result — mAL subtypes preferentially relaying multimodal sensory information — plausibly is that substrate, and it survives a WG3↔WG4 swap untouched.

So: name types by their connectome names, carry receptor identity as a labelled overlay with explicit confidence tiers, run the swap-robustness pass, and the manuscript cannot be killed by killing the assignment. The GRASP experiment then upgrades the overlay from inference to measurement.

Turning the contact-chemosensory assignment from an argument into a measurement.
Florian Kämpf · August 2026

The problem

We inferred the labels from the circuit, then used the labels to explain the circuit.

  • Receptor identity for WG3 / WG4 / LgLG* was assigned from downstream partners — PPN1 for WG3, mAL for WG4.
  • The manuscript's thesis is downstream valence logic.
  • Supporting evidence: neurotransmitter prediction, morphology against GAL4 images, and wing→leg co-clustering.
  • No further connectome analysis can break this loop.

And one leg of it is weaker than we thought

PPN1 is not a ppk25+ marker.

PPN1 takes near-equal input from both families — a convergent integrator, not a marker. The two families also interdigitate across its whole dendrite (KS D ≈ 0.11). That sentence in the draft needs rewriting regardless of everything else.

The move

Stop defending the labels. Solve for them.

Rj = Σi xi·C[i,j] ⁄ Σi C[i,j]

  • C = connectome synapse counts. EM segmentation — not typing inference.
  • x = the receptor labels. The actual unknown.
  • R = measured by GRASP, because ppk25+ cells are a subset of ppk23+ cells: the ppk23 driver labels both families, the ppk25 driver only one.
  • Unknown GRASP efficiency k is common across targets and cancels in ratios. One target tells you nothing; two tell you everything.

The ask

Three tiers, pick a stopping point.

  • 2 targets — AN05B023b + c. Settles the global M/F swap. 17× and 121×.
  • 4 targets — add INXXX044, IN11A022, IN01B065, AN09B017g. Decouples wing from leg.
  • 8 targets — the D-optimal set. Every type solved individually.
  • Reference arm throughout — R56C09 / PPN1. Without it a negative is uninterpretable.

Main unknown: driver lines. R56C09 is confirmed. AN09B017e/f = vAB3 and AN13B002 = Dandelion are named and may have reagents. The rest need a NeuronBridge hunt.

Risk

The paper is safe either way.

  • Name types by connectome names; carry receptor identity as a labelled overlay with explicit confidence tiers.
  • Run the WG3↔WG4 swap robustness pass and report which conclusions depend on the call.
  • The headline — contact pheromone signals dominating volatile ones at mAL, the anatomical substrate of the Wang 2011 hierarchy — survives a swap untouched.

So the experiment is an upgrade, not a dependency. It turns the overlay from inference into measurement.
