Receptor types of contact chemosensory neurons

A test to find the receptor type of each sensory neuron type. Male CNS connectome, male-cns:v1.0.

Working page · Florian Kämpf · 11 August 2026 · Related pages: AN05B102, AN05B023b/c, AN09B017.

This page uses ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. Sentences are short. The voice is active. Each term has one meaning.

These Technical Names keep their usual meaning: neuron, synapse, connectome, receptor, sensory neuron, gustatory receptor neuron, ventral nerve cord, neuropil, hemilineage, GAL4, LexA, split-GAL4, GRASP, t-GRASP, puncta, ppk23, ppk25, and the cell type names (WG1 to WG4, LgLG1a to LgLG8, AN, IN and DN types).

1 · The problem

A collaborator gave a receptor type to each sensory neuron type. He used the downstream partners of these neurons to do this. But the paper makes a claim about the same downstream partners. This is a loop.

You cannot use the downstream partners two times. You cannot use them first to find the receptor type, and then again to show what the receptor type does. More connectome analysis does not break this loop. Only a measurement outside the connectome breaks it.

3 · The method

Do not defend the receptor types. Calculate them. The connectome gives the equations. A GRASP test gives the measurements.

The method uses one fact about the two drivers:

  • The ppk25 cells are a part of the ppk23 cells.
  • Thus the ppk23 driver shows both groups.
  • The ppk25 driver shows only one group.
  • The ratio of the two puncta counts gives R.

An unknown factor k changes the puncta count. The factor k is different for each driver. The factor k also changes with the target neuron, the neuropil and the dendrite. Thus you cannot compare two different target neurons directly.

4 · What the test can show

The test cannot give you all eight receptor types one by one.

You can write one equation for each target neuron. With eight target neurons you get eight equations. Eight equations and eight unknown receptor types look sufficient. The mathematics agrees: the equations are independent, and the condition number is 2.5, which is good.

But each equation has two more unknown values in it. The first is the factor k. The second tells you how the puncta count changes when the number of synapses is very high. You do not know either value. Thus you have eight equations and ten unknown values. Eight equations cannot give ten answers.

Therefore ask a smaller question. Ask if the wing types are correct, and ask if the leg types are correct. Two questions need only two measurements.

There are four possible answers, not two

Nobody thinks that all the receptor types are wrong. The important errors are in the wing to leg step.

Use one pair of neurons and two neuropils

Use one pair of target neurons. Measure each animal two times. Measure first in the neuropil Ov. Then measure in the neuropil LegNp T1 to T3. This gives two values, W and L. Section 5 gives the pairs that you can use. The numbers below are for the pair AN05B023b and AN05B023c.

W = [log Y(ppk25,c,Ov) − log Y(ppk23,c,Ov)] − [log Y(ppk25,b,Ov) − log Y(ppk23,b,Ov)]
L = [log Y(ppk25,c,Leg) − log Y(ppk23,c,Leg)] − [log Y(ppk25,b,Leg) − log Y(ppk23,b,Leg)]

Each value uses one neuropil, the same sensory neurons and the same two drivers. Thus the factor k goes away fully. Do not compare W with L.

ValueIf correctIf wrongShows
W+1.04−1.06 the wing types
L+1.07−1.15 the leg types

The values are log10 units. The signs of W and L give one of the four answers.

Do this first. The equation for R is correct only if the two drivers show exactly the eight sensory neuron types. But 417 sensory neurons in five types have no receptor type. Also, four of the eight types have no molecular evidence.

Find which neurons the drivers show. Use HCR-FISH or MCFO. This work costs less than the GRASP test. If the ppk23 driver shows more than 1.3 times or less than 0.7 times the correct number of cells, stop. Do not build the GRASP flies.

The ppk23-LexA in this laboratory is one example: FlyBase shows 0 stocks for the construct, but the stock is in the freezer. Ask other laboratories before you make a new reagent.

Make the ppk23-LexA stock clean

The stock is ;ppk23-LexA/CyO; 22B10-Gal4/TM3, Ser. Remove the 22B10-GAL4 transgene.

  • Cross the stock to a stock with two balancers.
  • Select the flies that have Ser. These flies have TM3,Ser and not 22B10-GAL4.
  • Also select the flies that have CyO but not Sp. These flies have ppk23-LexA.
  • Cross the brothers and sisters. Keep the new stock.

Ser is dominant. Thus you can do this step with your eyes. You do not need PCR. Keep the first stock. Do not change it.

Make the ppk25-GAL4 stock clean

The stock BDSC 93028 has one insert on chromosome 2 and one insert on chromosome 3. Remove the insert on chromosome 3. There is a second reason to do this. Two inserts make a stronger driver than one insert. The ppk23 stock has one insert. The test compares these two drivers. Thus the two stocks must have the same number of inserts.

Warning. The two inserts have the same marker. Thus you must use PCR to show that you removed the insert on chromosome 3.

The laboratory has BDSC 79039 and BDSC 79040. It does not have the other stocks.

Do not change to syb-GRASP only to make chromosome 3 free. The t-GRASP reagent puts each half at the correct part of the neuron. The pre half goes to the active zone. The post half goes to the dendrite. Thus two neurons that only touch each other do not make a signal.

The syb-GRASP reagent puts the post half on the full membrane. Thus two neurons that touch each other can make a signal. This test compares LgLG1a with LgLG1b, and these two sensory types put their synapses in almost the same place. Thus a false signal is a large risk here.

Use the reagents on chromosome 2 only if chromosome 3 blocks the experiment.

There are two other methods. Each one answers a different question:

  • The syb-GRASP reagent shows synapses that are active. It does not show all synapses. Thus it gives a second, different result.
  • The QUAS reagent gives a third binary system. Use it if you must show three neuron groups in one animal.

10 · The paper is safe

Use the connectome names in the paper. Write WG3, WG4, LgLG1a and LgLG1b. Show the receptor types as an extra layer. Give a confidence level to each receptor type.

Then change WG3 and WG4 and calculate the results again. Show which results change. Wang and his group showed in 2011 that contact pheromones control the fly more than volatile pheromones. The mAL result in Figure 5 possibly shows how the brain does this. This result does not change if WG3 and WG4 change.
